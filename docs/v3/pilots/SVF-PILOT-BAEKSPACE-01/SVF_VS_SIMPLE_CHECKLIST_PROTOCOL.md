# SVF-PILOT-BAEKSPACE-01 — SVF_VS_SIMPLE_CHECKLIST_PROTOCOL

**Pilot ID:** SVF-PILOT-BAEKSPACE-01  
**As-of:** 2026-09-19  
**Purpose:** test whether full SVF adds enough value over a lightweight decision checklist.

---

## 1. Why this comparison exists

SVF fails commercially if a one-page checklist produces materially the same decision quality with much less effort.

Therefore BaekSpace Pilot must compare SVF against a deliberately competent simple baseline.

The baseline is **not** a strawman.

---

## 2. Frozen simple checklist

Give the evaluator only the frozen pre-SVF `BASELINE_PACKET.md` and this checklist:

1. What decision must be made now?
2. What do we know?
3. What do we not know?
4. What are the realistic options?
5. What is the biggest risk/assumption behind each?
6. What is the cheapest next test?
7. What result would make us continue, stop, or change direction?
8. What should we avoid building before that evidence exists?

No SVF vocabulary is supplied.

---

## 3. Comparison conditions

Preferred:

- independent reviewer or separate LLM session;
- same baseline evidence snapshot;
- no access to Navigation Map, Evidence Ledger, Hypothesis Register, or Method Route;
- time spent recorded.

If the same analyst performs both, mark the comparison as contaminated by learning.

---

## 4. Compare outputs dimension by dimension

Do not create one total score yet.

Compare:

- material Unknowns surfaced;
- options identified;
- whether D or another new option emerges;
- unsupported claims;
- competing hypotheses;
- evidence/assumption separation;
- smallest experiment quality;
- stop/pivot conditions;
- method overkill avoided;
- traceability;
- time/cognitive burden;
- ease of explaining output to another person.

---

## 5. SVF incremental-value rule

SVF earns continued complexity only if it repeatedly adds one or more of:

- materially important Unknown missed by checklist;
- decision option missed by checklist;
- evidence/hypothesis distinction that changes action;
- better experiment definition;
- useful trigger/pivot rule;
- better later reconstruction/adaptation.

Better terminology or longer documentation alone does not count.

---

## 6. Simplification trigger

If the checklist reaches the same actionable result with materially less burden:

> **downgrade this decision class to Quick Lens or checklist use.**

If repeated across pilots:

> remove unnecessary SVF layers from the Stage 3 Core.

---

## 7. Status

Protocol frozen.

Comparison has **not yet been run**.

A fair comparison should occur before claiming the BaekSpace pilot validates SVF.
