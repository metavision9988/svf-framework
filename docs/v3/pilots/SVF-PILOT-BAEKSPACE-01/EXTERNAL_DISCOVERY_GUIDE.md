# SVF-PILOT-BAEKSPACE-01 — EXTERNAL_DISCOVERY_GUIDE

**As-of:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** Execution guide  
**Use with:** Core experiment + Studio proxy

---

## 1. Interview rule

> **Problem first. Product second.**

Do not begin by explaining why BaekSpace is special.

Do not ask:

- “Do you have trouble managing AI output?”
- “Would privacy be valuable?”
- “Would a Today feature help?”
- “Would you pay for this?”

Those questions manufacture agreement.

---

## 2. Opening

Suggested neutral opening:

> “I’m looking at how people keep and return to AI-generated material, and how they manage unfinished ideas or projects. I’m more interested in what you actually do now than in opinions about a new product.”

Ask permission to take anonymous notes.

Do not collect unnecessary personal data.

---

## 3. Current-behavior questions

Ask for recent concrete examples.

### AI/document flow

- Think of the last AI answer you wanted to keep. What happened next?
- Where did you put it?
- Did you reopen it? Show or describe when.
- What did you do with it after reopening?
- What gets lost or forgotten?
- Which material do you avoid putting into another online service?
- What is your normal tool for this?

### Unfinished-work flow

- What unfinished things are active right now?
- Where are they written down?
- How do you choose what to do today?
- Tell me about something important that stayed unfinished.
- What caused it to stall?
- When you return after a few days, how do you recover context?
- Which tool or routine helps most? What is still annoying?

Do not introduce Core/Studio labels yet.

---

## 4. Fit classification

After the behavior discussion, classify:

- Core fit;
- Studio fit;
- both;
- neither.

“Neither” is a useful result.

Do not force product exposure when the job is absent.

---

## 5. Core exposure

For Core-fit users:

> “Here is the current tool. Use one real document you care about in whatever way makes sense to you.”

Avoid a feature tour.

If they become stuck, distinguish:

- comprehension issue;
- UX issue;
- missing blocker;
- unsupported use case.

Record facilitator intervention.

---

## 6. Studio proxy exposure

For Studio-fit users:

Explain only the four objects:

- Project
- Topic
- Next Action
- Today

Then use the participant's real work.

Do not pitch the future roadmap.

Do not tell them the intended answer is to “finish more.”

Observe whether the structure itself is useful.

---

## 7. Follow-up

At the end:

> “Use it only if you actually need it. I’ll contact you later to ask what happened.”

During follow-up ask:

- Did you use it again?
- What triggered the return?
- What did you accomplish?
- What did you ignore?
- What was annoying enough to avoid?
- What did you use instead?
- If this disappeared, what would you do?

Avoid asking only for satisfaction.

---

## 8. Commercial discovery questions

Only after behavior is understood:

- Does anyone in your team currently spend money/time solving this?
- Is this an individual inconvenience or a team/business problem?
- Who would decide whether to adopt a new workflow/tool?
- What would need to be true before you tried it with real work?
- What security/integration concerns would stop adoption?

Pricing questions are optional and weak at this stage.

Actual commitment is stronger evidence than stated price.

---

## 9. Evidence hygiene

After each session, write notes before discussing conclusions with other reviewers.

Separate:

- direct observation;
- participant quote/paraphrase;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis update.

Do not convert “interesting” into “important.”
